5 reports read as a one-day move: counted under parser, where the desk files them, and again as shift.

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so day and month swap: 7 April goes out as 4 July. Reports come in by app, email and form, from all eleven tenants. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: booking-v3’s date hint says MM/DD/YYYY

Tenants on booking-v3 print MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only reports that came in through the form of a booking-v3 tenant belong here. The parser fix does not touch the form or its hint. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

shift: the date moves by one day

Some reports are not a swap at all. The date is one day off: Tuesday 12 May shows as Monday 11 May, a meeting lands on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, every booking in June is one day off, an invite arrives before the booking was made. The desk noted these as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), and causes.ts still files them under parser by tenant. A one-day move looks like a time-zone or daylight-saving bug, not a date-order bug. The dd/mm parser fix does not touch it, so this ticket stays open.

How the counts are made

  • One id, one report: the export repeats some reports, and they are counted once.
  • hint and parser are counted the way the desk counts them, from causes.ts: the tenant’s locale and booking form, and the channel the report came in by. What intake tagged a report as does not count.
  • shift is every report the desk noted as a one-day move. They stay counted under parser, where the desk files them, so the three counts add up to more than the total.

Lessons

  • Merging by subject line put three problems into one ticket. Read the bodies before you call a ticket known.
  • A note that a report reads differently is a sign it may be its own problem.
  • Closing the merged ticket is not closing every problem in it. Check each one against what shipped.
